# Project Datasets

This directory serves as the centralized repository for all tabular datasets, metadata, and assets utilized across the learning modules and notebooks in this workspace.

## Root Datasets Catalog

*   **`area_prices_monthly.csv`**: Monthly housing prices and square footage trends across different neighborhoods.
*   **`finalairways.csv`**: Air traffic passenger statistics, routes, and schedules.
*   **`finalbangloreroadways.csv`** & **`finaldelhiroadways.csv`**: Traffic metrics, travel speeds, and road route details for Bangalore and Delhi.
*   **`finalrailways.csv`**: Indian Railways schedules, station mappings, and passenger stats.
*   **`ford.csv`**: Ford automotive specs, models, mileage, fuel types, and prices.
*   **`heart.csv`**: Clinical data for predicting cardiovascular diseases (age, cholesterol, heart rate, diagnosis labels).
*   **`intercitybuses.csv`**: Transit records and ticket fares of intercity bus lines.
*   **`ipl_data.csv`**: Indian Premier League statistics, runs scored, wickets, teams, and match outcomes.
*   **`Iris.csv`**: Classic flower classification taxonomy dataset containing sepal/petal dimensions of setosa, versicolor, and virginica.
*   **`netflix_titles.csv`**: Metadata catalog of all movies and TV shows available on Netflix.
*   **`train.csv`**: Standard training partition split.

## Doraemon Dataset Subfolder

Located in **[`doremon dataset/`](file:///d:/python/datasets/doremon%20dataset/)**, this contains a rich collection of tables and images centered around the Doraemon anime/manga franchise:

### 1. Tabular Metadata
*   **`character_list.csv`**: Character names (original Japanese and English localizations), species, birthdates, ages, and primary roles.
*   **`episodes_list.csv`**: List of episodes including titles, season numbers, duration, air date, summary plots, and key gadgets featured.
*   **`gadget_list.csv`**: Catalog of futuristic secret gadgets from Doraemon.
*   **`movie_list.csv`**: Release year, box office revenue, director, and studio records of Doraemon theatrical movies.
*   **`songs_list.csv`**: Theme songs, lyrics, artists, and year of release.

### 2. Images Catalog
The **[`doremon dataset/images/`](file:///d:/python/datasets/doremon%20dataset/images/)** directory holds categorized profile images of main characters used for computer vision and classification tasks:
*   **`Doraemon/`**: Profile photos and illustrations of Doraemon.
*   **`Nobita/`**: Character assets of Nobita Nobi.
*   **`Shizuka/`**: Character assets of Shizuka Minamoto.
*   **`Suneo/`**: Character assets of Suneo Honekawa.
*   **`Gian/`**: Character assets of Takeshi "Gian" Goda.
*   **`Test/`**: Unlabeled testing images.
